## Safety Notice

**This device is NOT a BIS-certified life-safety device.**

Under Indian regulations, gas detection equipment used for safety purposes must conform to **IS/IEC 60079-29** series standards, which cover performance requirements for flammable gas detectors, guidance on functional safety, and selection, installation, use and maintenance of such systems. This project has **not** been tested against any of these standards.

It must **not** be relied upon as the sole means of detecting gas leaks in any residential, commercial, or industrial setting. It has not been certified or approved by BIS, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, the Ministry of Labour and Employment, or any other Indian regulatory body.

**Recent regulatory context:** The Government of Maharashtra has issued directives mandating that buildings install **BIS-certified gas leak detection systems with automatic shut-off mechanisms**, with municipal authorities verifying these systems before issuing completion or occupancy certificates. This project does **not** meet those requirements.

Users in India are strongly advised to:

- Install **BIS-certified gas detectors** (as mandated in some Indian states) alongside this device
- Follow all **Indian gas safety regulations**, including those published by the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO)
- Consult your **LPG distributor** (Indian Oil, HP Gas, Bharat Gas) for safety guidance and certified equipment
- Follow **LPG safety tips** issued by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas and Indian Oil Corporation, including using BIS-approved regulators and Suraksha rubber tubes from authorised distributors only
- **Never** rely solely on this device for personal, family, or property safety
- Consult a **qualified professional** before using this device in any critical application
